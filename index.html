Before you use QuietChat

QuietChat is a web app, not iMessage. Chat messages and shared attachments are currently stored in the online Supabase service so your chat partners can receive them. This update also saves copies of media you view in this browser.

  • Local media stays on this browser/device and may be lost if browser data is cleared.
  • Local copies do not automatically appear on another computer.
  • Only chat with people you trust. Do not send passwords or highly sensitive information.
  • Calls use Talkvoo in a separate browser tab; call privacy depends on that service and its settings.
