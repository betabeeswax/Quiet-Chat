QUIETCHAT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

QuietChat is a small private-chat app. This policy explains what the current version stores and what its limits are. It is a project policy, not legal advice.

Information stored

  • Your chosen username, optional bio, and optional profile picture.
  • Chat membership, invite metadata, messages, timestamps, and uploaded attachments.
  • Game scores and moves for the in-chat rock-paper-scissors game.
  • Supabase may process technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

Ways to sign in

You can sign in with email and password, use Google if configured, create a username-and-password account without providing an email address, or continue as a guest. For username-and-password accounts, QuietChat uses an internal non-deliverable address for Supabase authentication; it is not a real email address and cannot receive password reset messages. Choose a strong password and keep it safe. There is no email-based password recovery for these accounts. Guest accounts are anonymous and may be lost if browser data is cleared or the session is lost.

Who can see your content?

I, the app creator, do not intend to personally read users’ emails or private chats. QuietChat is not end-to-end encrypted, however, and I cannot promise that messages are technically inaccessible to service administrators or infrastructure providers. Messages and attachments are intended to be visible to members of that chat; Supabase processes the data to operate the service. If someone reports abuse or a safety issue, relevant information may need to be reviewed to investigate it. Do not use QuietChat for secrets or highly sensitive information.

Files and exports

Files attached to messages and profile pictures are limited to 5 MiB each. Attachments are stored privately, and the app creates temporary links to view or download them. A PDF export opens a print-friendly transcript; choose “Save as PDF” in the browser. Messages and call links are included as text, while attachments appear as temporary links and are not embedded inside the PDF. Export links can expire.

Calls and external services

Video calls open Talkvoo in a new tab, and the call URL is posted in the chat. Talkvoo is a separate service with its own privacy terms. Do not share a call link publicly. QuietChat does not provide end-to-end encryption guarantees for calls.

Retention and deletion

Guest accounts are intended to be deleted after seven days without activity. QuietChat records recent activity when you use the app and runs a scheduled server cleanup daily. If you are a guest, create a registered account to keep your account beyond the guest period. The database is also configured to schedule a yearly cleanup: messages older than one year and chats created more than one year ago are deleted. Cleanup depends on the scheduled database job being available and enabled. Backups or provider logs may retain data for their own limited periods.

Guest account limits

Guests can chat in chats they have access to, but cannot create groups or communities, remove chats or leave groups from their account, or join 18+ groups. Create an account to manage groups and your chat list. Guest data may be lost if browser data is cleared.

Two-factor authentication

This app does not require two-factor authentication. Username-only anonymous sessions do not offer the same account protection as a recoverable account with strong credentials.

Children and sensitive information

Use a nickname and avoid posting addresses, phone numbers, passwords, school details, financial information, or other private information. Only chat with people you trust.

Changes

This policy may change as the app changes. The current policy will be published with the app files.
